Games 1 through 15: Umm can I have infinite time please?

July 18, 2026 y games

Stats

Two ten minute warm up games with a friend to orient me on the 19x19 board, thirty minutes before my first scheduled live game with a stranger from a discord league. 1 live game, 30 min main time ( of which I used all of my 30 minutes and my opponent used 5)

What surprised me?

How hard it is to think under time pressure.

How did my perception change?

I thought I will play badly because I'm a beginner but I played even worse because of the timer. A part of the practice and training will have to be to reduce number of decisions and thinking / attempts at thinking.

Current hypothesis

I take too much time in early game, increasing pressure for myself in mid game which is when things are more confusing. I need to manage time first, then the stress in the second half.
