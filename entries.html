Before the first 19x19

There will always be a voice that says "Why didn't I start 10 years ago or 20 years ago??!" Some grief about lost time and delayed paths will always remain.

The truth is not that I didn't try but that I tried and failed repeatedly, just not in ways that leads to progressive learning.

The failures and barriers were all the same across various domains I hold this grief for... art, aerials, climbing, and go.

If I'm being more compassionate with myself then I would say in those years I was becoming the kind of person who would eventually play go and be eager to see the mirror that it is.

2007 - I first learned about the game and its origins through an IEEE computing article. IEEE announced it as the next big challenge for the computer sci world.
2011 - I bought my first go board and procrastinated actually learning.
2015 - I met three go players in a software immersive program I was in. On gave me my first lesson (JR) and the second (KB) became one of my closest friends continued to teach me the game.
This first year of playing I watched the original hikaru no go, played a 100 games on 9x9, 10x10, and a few on 13x13
2021-2025 - Zero games. Thinking of go and just feeling "Not now".
2026 - I watched the chinese live action adaptation of hikaru no go and also found myself in the first period of stability (health) in years and had enough capacity to so I began again.
